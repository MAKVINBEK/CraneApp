FAQ
How do I delete my account? ▼
Go to Settings → Account → Delete account. This action cannot be undone and deletes all your data.
How do I enable two-factor authentication? ▼
Go to Settings → Privacy → Two-factor authentication and follow the instructions.
How do I block a user? ▼
Open the user profile, tap the three dots, and choose "Block".
How do I create a group? ▼
Tap the create button on the main screen and choose "Create group".
